# Northwind Dynamics — Remote & Hybrid Work Policy

**Policy owner:** People Operations
**Effective:** 1 February 2025

## 1. Working models

| Model | Definition |
|---|---|
| Office-based | In a company office 4–5 days per week |
| Hybrid (default) | Minimum **2 days per week** in the office, by team agreement |
| Fully remote | No routine office attendance; approved case-by-case |

- The default for most roles is **Hybrid**.
- Fully remote status requires **VP approval** and is reviewed every **12 months**.

## 2. Eligibility

- Employees must have completed **probation** to request fully remote status.
- The role must be one that can be performed effectively without a physical presence.
- Performance rating must be **3 or above** at the last review.

## 3. Core hours and availability

- Be online and responsive during **core hours 10:00–16:00** in your team's primary time zone.
- Keep your calendar current and your status accurate.
- Camera-on is expected for external meetings and 1:1s unless agreed otherwise.

## 4. Equipment and stipend

- The company provides a **laptop, monitor, keyboard and mouse**.
- A one-time **home office stipend of USD 500** is available to set up a safe, ergonomic workspace; claim via Expensify with receipts within **90 days** of your start date or of approval for remote work.
- The monthly internet contribution is **USD 30**, added to payroll for fully remote employees.

## 5. Security expectations

- Work only on company-managed devices, over the **corporate VPN** (see IT Security Policy).
- Ensure family members and visitors cannot see or access company screens or documents.
- Do not print Confidential or Restricted material at home.

## 6. Working from abroad

- Working outside your country of employment is permitted for a maximum of **20 working days per year** and must be approved in advance (tax and immigration review).
- Permanent relocation to another country is treated as a new employment arrangement.

## 7. Expenses

Home office furniture beyond the stipend, utilities and rent are **not** reimbursable. Co-working day passes may be reimbursed up to **USD 250 per month** with manager approval.
