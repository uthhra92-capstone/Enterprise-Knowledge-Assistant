# Northwind Dynamics — Company FAQ

A quick-reference companion to the full policies. Where this FAQ and a policy
disagree, the **policy document is authoritative**.

## Payroll & pay

**When am I paid?** Monthly, on the **25th**. If the 25th is a weekend or public holiday, you are paid on the preceding working day.

**Where are my payslips?** In the HR portal (Workday) by the **26th** of each month.

**Who do I contact about a payroll error?** Email **payroll@northwind.example** within 5 working days of the payslip date.

## IT & accounts

**How do I reset my password?** Go to **passwordreset.northwind.example**, verify with MFA, and set a new password (minimum 14 characters). If you are locked out, call the Service Desk at **x4357**.

**How do I get software installed?** Raise a request in the Service Desk portal under *Software → New request*. Unapproved self-installs are blocked by policy.

**I lost my laptop — what now?** Report it to the Service Desk **within 1 hour** so it can be wiped remotely. File a police report if it was stolen.

## Time off

**How much annual leave do I get?** 20 days per year (25 after 5 years' service). See the Leave Policy.

**Can I carry unused leave into next year?** Up to **5 days**, and they must be used by **31 March**.

**How do I request leave?** Workday → Time Off, at least **10 working days** in advance for planned leave.

## Facilities

**Office hours:** Offices are badge-accessible **07:00–20:00** on weekdays. After-hours access needs manager approval.

**Public holidays 2025 (HQ):** 1 Jan, 18 Apr, 21 Apr, 26 May, 4 Jul, 1 Sep, 27 Nov, 25 Dec, 26 Dec. Locations observe their local statutory holidays.

**Visitors:** Register guests in the lobby system at least **1 day** ahead; guests must be escorted at all times.

## People & support

**Who is my HR contact?** Your assigned People Partner is listed in Workday under *My Team → HR Support*.

**Ethics Hotline:** Portal → "Speak Up" (anonymous option available).

**Employee Assistance Programme:** 24/7 confidential counselling, 6 sessions per issue per year — details in the Benefits Guide.
