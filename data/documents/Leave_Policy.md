# Northwind Dynamics — Leave Policy

**Policy owner:** People Operations
**Effective:** 1 January 2025
**Applies to:** All full-time and part-time employees

## 1. Annual leave (paid vacation)

- Full-time employees accrue **20 working days** of annual leave per calendar year.
- Accrual is **1.67 days per completed month** of service.
- Employees with **5 or more years** of continuous service receive **25 working days** per year.
- Part-time employees accrue leave on a pro-rata basis according to contracted hours.

## 2. Carry-forward

- Up to **5 unused annual leave days** may be carried forward into the next calendar year.
- Carried-forward days must be used by **31 March**; any balance above 5 days or not used by 31 March is forfeited without payment.
- Carry-forward beyond 5 days may be approved **only** by a Director where business needs prevented leave from being taken.

## 3. Leave encashment

- On resignation or retirement, **accrued but unused** annual leave (current year plus valid carry-forward) is paid out with the final salary.
- Encashment during employment is **not permitted** except where required by local law.

## 4. Sick leave

- Employees are entitled to **10 paid sick days** per calendar year.
- A **medical certificate** is required for any absence of **3 or more consecutive days**.
- Unused sick leave **does not** carry forward and is **not** encashable.

## 5. Public holidays

- Employees receive all **gazetted public holidays** for their work location (see the Company FAQ for the current-year list).
- Where a public holiday falls on a weekend, the following working day is observed.

## 6. Applying for leave

1. Submit the request in the HR portal (**Workday → Time Off**) at least **10 working days** in advance for planned leave.
2. Your line manager approves or declines within **3 working days**.
3. For sick or emergency leave, notify your manager **by 10:00 on the first day** of absence and log it in the portal on return.

## 7. Other leave types

| Leave type | Entitlement |
|---|---|
| Bereavement leave | 5 paid days (immediate family) |
| Jury duty | Paid for the duration of service |
| Unpaid personal leave | Up to 30 days at management discretion |
| Marriage leave | 3 paid days |

Parental leave is covered separately in the **Benefits Guide**.
