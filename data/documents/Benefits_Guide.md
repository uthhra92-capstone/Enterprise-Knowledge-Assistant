# Northwind Dynamics — Benefits Guide

**Policy owner:** People Operations — Total Rewards
**Plan year:** 2025

## 1. Health insurance

- Company-paid medical, dental and vision cover for the employee.
- Dependants (spouse/partner and children) can be added; the company pays **70%** of the dependant premium, the employee pays **30%** via payroll deduction.
- Coverage starts on the employee's **first day**. Changes are allowed at annual open enrolment (**November**) or within **30 days** of a qualifying life event (marriage, birth, adoption, loss of other cover).

## 2. Retirement savings

- **401(k)** (or local equivalent pension) with company match of **100% of the first 4%** and **50% of the next 2%** of eligible pay — a maximum match of **5%**.
- Employee contributions vest immediately; the company match vests after **2 years** of service.

## 3. Parental leave

- **Primary caregiver:** 16 weeks of fully paid leave.
- **Secondary caregiver:** 6 weeks of fully paid leave.
- Must be taken within **12 months** of birth or adoption; can be taken in up to **3 blocks**.
- Eligibility: **6 months** of continuous service before the leave start date.
- A phased return at 60–80% hours for the first 4 weeks is available on request.

## 4. Wellness allowance

- **USD 600 per year** reimbursable for gym membership, fitness classes, mental-health apps, ergonomic equipment or nutrition programmes.
- Claim through Expensify with a receipt; unused allowance does not carry over.

## 5. Learning & development

- **USD 1,500 per year** for courses, conferences, books and certifications relevant to your role.
- Manager approval required before purchase; claims via Expensify.

## 6. Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP)

- Purchase company shares via payroll deduction of **1–15%** of salary.
- **15% discount** on the lower of the price at the start or end of each **6-month** offering period.
- Enrolment windows are **May** and **November**.

## 7. Other benefits

| Benefit | Detail |
|---|---|
| Life insurance | 3× annual salary, company-paid |
| Employee Assistance Programme | 24/7 confidential counselling, 6 sessions/issue/year |
| Commuter benefit | Up to USD 150/month pre-tax |
| Home office stipend | One-time USD 500 (see Remote Work Policy) |
| Referral bonus | USD 3,000 per successful hire after they pass probation |
