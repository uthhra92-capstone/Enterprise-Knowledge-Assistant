# Northwind Dynamics — Code of Conduct

**Policy owner:** Legal & Compliance
**Effective:** 1 January 2025

## 1. Our standards

Every employee, contractor and board member must act with **honesty, integrity and respect**, comply with all applicable laws, and protect the company's assets and reputation.

## 2. Anti-harassment and discrimination

- Harassment, bullying, intimidation and discrimination are strictly prohibited.
- This covers conduct in the office, at company events, on business travel and on company communication channels.
- Reports can be made to any manager, to People Operations, or anonymously via the **Ethics Hotline** (portal → "Speak Up"). Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is itself a serious violation.

## 3. Conflicts of interest

- Disclose any outside activity, investment or relationship that could conflict with your duties (for example, a family member at a supplier, or a second job with a competitor).
- Disclosures go to Legal & Compliance via the **Conflicts Register** and must be updated within **30 days** of any change.

## 4. Gifts and entertainment

- You may accept unsolicited gifts of modest value up to **USD 100** per source per year.
- Gifts of **cash or cash equivalents** (gift cards, vouchers) may never be accepted or given.
- Anything above USD 100, or any gift involving a government official, requires **written pre-approval** from Legal & Compliance.

## 5. Anti-bribery and corruption

- Zero tolerance for bribery, kickbacks or facilitation payments, whether offered, promised, given or received, directly or through a third party.
- All third-party intermediaries must pass due diligence before engagement.

## 6. Confidentiality and data protection

- Protect confidential company, customer and employee information both during and after employment.
- Only collect and use personal data for legitimate business purposes and in line with the IT Security Policy and privacy law.

## 7. Use of company resources

- Company funds, equipment, systems and time are for business purposes.
- Accurate books and records must be kept; never falsify a document, timesheet or expense claim.

## 8. Raising concerns and consequences

- If something seems wrong, **speak up**. Good-faith reports are protected.
- Breaches of this Code may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination, and may be referred to authorities where the law requires.
