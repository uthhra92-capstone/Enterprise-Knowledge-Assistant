# Northwind Dynamics — HR Handbook

**Policy owner:** People Operations
**Version:** 3.2 (2025)

## 1. Working hours

- Standard week is **40 hours**, Monday to Friday.
- **Core hours** are **10:00–16:00**; start and end times are flexible around them.
- Overtime for non-exempt staff is paid at **1.5×** and must be pre-approved in writing.

## 2. Probation

- New employees serve a **6-month probation** period.
- During probation either party may terminate with **2 weeks' notice**.
- A probation review meeting is held at months **3** and **6**.

## 3. Performance reviews

- Formal reviews occur **twice a year** (April and October).
- Ratings run from **1 (below expectations)** to **5 (outstanding)**.
- Merit increases and bonuses are decided in the **April** cycle and paid with **May** salary.

## 4. Compensation and payroll

- Salaries are paid **monthly**, on the **25th**; if the 25th is a weekend or holiday, payment is the preceding working day.
- Payslips are available in the HR portal by the **26th**.
- Salary reviews happen annually; ad-hoc adjustments require VP approval.

## 5. Grievance procedure

1. Raise the concern informally with your line manager.
2. If unresolved within **10 working days**, submit a **formal written grievance** to People Operations.
3. A grievance hearing is held within **15 working days**; the outcome is provided in writing.
4. You may appeal within **5 working days** of the outcome.

## 6. Resignation and notice periods

| Level | Notice period |
|---|---|
| During probation | 2 weeks |
| Individual contributor | 4 weeks |
| Manager / Senior | 8 weeks |
| Director and above | 12 weeks |

- Notice must be given in writing to your manager and People Operations.
- Accrued unused annual leave is paid out; company equipment must be returned on the last working day.

## 7. Equal opportunity

Northwind Dynamics is an equal-opportunity employer. Employment decisions are based on merit, qualifications and business need, without regard to race, gender, age, religion, disability, sexual orientation or any other protected characteristic.
