# Northwind Dynamics — Travel & Expense Policy

**Policy owner:** Finance
**Effective:** 1 January 2025

## 1. Booking travel

- All business travel must be **approved by your manager in advance** and booked through the corporate travel tool (**TravelPerk**).
- Book at least **14 days ahead** where possible to control cost.
- Choose the lowest logical fare/rate; loyalty points earned on business travel belong to the employee.

## 2. Air travel class

| Flight duration | Permitted class |
|---|---|
| Under 6 hours | Economy |
| 6 hours or more | Premium economy |
| 9 hours or more (Director+ only) | Business |

Upgrades using personal funds or miles are allowed at the employee's own cost.

## 3. Accommodation

- Standard limit: **USD 200 per night** (before tax) in most cities; **USD 300** in designated high-cost cities (New York, London, San Francisco, Tokyo, Singapore, Zurich).
- Breakfast is reimbursable if not included in the room rate.

## 4. Per diem (meals & incidentals)

- **Domestic travel:** USD 60 per day.
- **International travel:** USD 100 per day.
- Per diem is **halved** on the first and last day of a trip.
- No receipts are required for per diem; itemised meal claims instead of per diem are **not** accepted.

## 5. Ground transport

- Use standard taxi/ride-share or public transport. Premium ride tiers are not reimbursed.
- Personal car mileage is reimbursed at **USD 0.67 per mile**.
- Rental cars: mid-size or smaller unless three or more colleagues travel together.

## 6. Non-reimbursable expenses

Personal entertainment, minibar, in-flight Wi-Fi over USD 25 per flight, spa, fines and penalties, travel companions' costs, and hotel room upgrades.

## 7. Submitting expenses

1. Submit claims in **Expensify within 30 days** of the expense being incurred.
2. Attach itemised receipts for every line item **over USD 25**.
3. Manager approval is required; Finance reimburses approved claims in the **next payroll run** (paid on the 25th).
4. Claims submitted more than **60 days** late require VP approval and may be declined.

## 8. Corporate cards

- Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel more than **4 times per year**.
- Card statements must be reconciled in Expensify **monthly**; unreconciled items may be deducted from salary.
